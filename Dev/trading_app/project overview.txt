		Phase 2 - Automated Trading Bot Development:
	•	Objective: Build an automated trading bot based on the insights gained from strategy testing.
	•	Strategy Integration: Incorporate successful strategies and indicators into the bot's decision-making algorithms.
	•	Automation and Real-Time Processing: Ensure the bot can execute trades automatically based on predefined criteria and real-time market data.
