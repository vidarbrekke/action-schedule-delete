In case you are wondering what the consensus strategy is, it is as follows:
Once all the SMA strategies have been evaluated, one will count how many of the concur in a buy or sell decision at any given point in time. In the event that more than 50% of them agree on a specific action, the Consensus strategy will execute a trade accordingly.
There is also a cooldown parameter which tells the consensus algorithm to halt trading for a specific period of time after a trade has been made, as to not perform multiple trades in short succession.
