# TuneTussle User Guide (Player)

Welcome to TuneTussle! This guide will help you join and play a real-time music quiz as a participant.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Get a Game Code:**
   - Ask the Judge (host) for the 6-character game code.

2. **Join the Game:**
   - Open the TuneTussle app in your browser (mobile or desktop).
   - Enter the game code and your name on the Home page.
   - Tap **Join Game**.

3. **Wait in the Lobby:**
   - You'll see a list of all participants.
   - Wait for the Judge to start the game.

---

## Gameplay Flow

1. **Listen to the Song:**
   - The Judge will play a song snippet on their device (audio is not streamed).
   - A YouTube link for the song is shown to the Judge for playback. (The system supports different music providers.)

2. **Buzz In:**
   - When you know the answer, tap the **Buzz** button as fast as you can!
   - If you're first, you'll be prompted to answer.

3. **Submit Your Answer:**
   - Type your guess (song title and/or artist) and submit.
   - If you're correct, you'll earn points!
   - If you're wrong or time runs out, others can buzz in.

4. **View Scores:**
   - After each round, scores are updated in real time.
   - The correct answer is shown to all players.

5. **Game Over:**
   - After all rounds, the final scores and winner(s) are displayed.
   - You can play again or join a new game.

---

## Tips & FAQ

- **Is audio streamed to my device?**
  - No. The Judge plays the song on their device. Listen in the same room or over a call.
- **What if the YouTube link is missing or invalid?**
  - The Judge may play the song manually. The system supports multiple music providers.
- **What if I lose connection?**
  - The app will automatically attempt to reconnect and restore your game state.
  - If automatic reconnection fails, refresh the page and rejoin with the same code and name.
- **Can I change my name?**
  - Leave and rejoin with a new name.
- **How are answers scored?**
  - Points for correct song, artist, or both. The Judge may override scores if needed.
- **Is there a time limit?**
  - Yes, you must answer quickly after buzzing in.
- **What do error messages mean?**
  - The app now provides clear, actionable error messages. Follow the suggested actions when errors occur.
  - Common error messages include invalid game codes, connection issues, or timeout notifications.

---

Enjoy the game and good luck!
